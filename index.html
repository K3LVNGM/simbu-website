The Runner

Born to run.
Built to endure.

“The last 100 meters don't belong to your legs. They belong to your heart.”

From the red-dirt roads of Arusha to the world stage in Tokyo, Alphonce Felix Simbu has built a career defined by grit, patience, and the relentless pursuit of excellence. A national hero who runs not just for medals, but for the future of Tanzanian athletics.

The Foundation

Running for something bigger than a finish line
