Kimbia na Simbu

/ kim-bee-ah nah sim-boo / — “Run with Simbu”

“Kimbia na Simbu” — Run with Simbu — is a campaign to fund youth training programs, athletics equipment, and educational scholarships across Tanzania. Every contribution, no matter the size, fuels the next generation of champions.

“Kimbia na Simbu” ni kampeni ya kuchangia mafunzo ya vijana, vifaa vya michezo, na ufadhili wa masomo kote Tanzania. Kila mchango, hata kiasi kidogo, unasaidia kuandaa mabingwa wa kizazi kijacho.

Transparency

How Your Support Helps

40%

Youth Training Programs

30%

Education Scholarships

Equipment & Gear

Running shoes, training kit, and sports facility improvements

10%

Community Events

Grassroots running events and community health programs

100% of donations go directly to the Alphonce Simbu Foundation. Full transparency reports are published annually.

Impact

Your support creates champions

Every shilling contributed to Kimbia na Simbu goes directly to the Alphonce Simbu Foundation, funding the programs that turn potential into performance. From the first pair of running shoes to race day registration, your support makes the difference.
