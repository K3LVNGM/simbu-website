## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored in the top 900px; template below it — roughly 60/40.**

Genuinely authored: the hero lockup (`clamp(2.9rem, 8.5vw, 7.5rem)` at `line-height: 1.04`, `-0.02em`, split into two block spans with the payload word carrying the gold `<em>`) reads as a poster, not a headline, and holds at 390px. The `.stats` strip builds hairlines from `gap: 1px` over a translucent background rather than borders — a results board, the right genre reference. `.split__media::after`'s offset gold frame, the M-Pesa `.donate` panel, the `.steps` counter list, and `.hero__pron` are nobody's template. Palette discipline is the reason it reads expensive: gold appears in six specific roles and is never decoration.

Pure template slots: the `.eyebrow` reading "World Champion" above every `<h1>` on all six pages — including "World Champion / Get in Touch" and "World Champion / Gallery" — is a variable nobody varied, and the loudest tell on the site. Also `.press__list` (eight unlinked brand names), the "Stay in the Race" newsletter block, the contact `.info` triptych, and the entire footer. The 4-up icon grid on foundation and the 4-up `.card__pct` grid on kimbia are the same module twice with numerals swapped.

Specificity evaporates below the homepage fold. Career, gallery and contact each open with a flat ink block and never recover a distinctive moment.

**Deterministic scan.** CLI detector: exit 2, 7 findings — `overused-font` ×6 (all citing the same Google Fonts link; owner-dismissed) and `flat-type-hierarchy` ×1. **Net: zero confirmed site defects from the CLI.**

Two mechanical notes worth keeping:
- `detect.mjs` does not read `.impeccable/config.json` — only the edit hook does. The owner's font dismissal is real but invisible to the CLI, so every future CLI run re-reports those six.
- `flat-type-hierarchy` at kimbia-na-simbu.html:137 is a **false positive** with a traceable cause: the rule reads computed font sizes under jsdom, which never fetches the linked stylesheet, so the only resolvable sizes are inline. kimbia is the one page with inline `font-size` — exactly two declarations — and the reported `{15.5, 22.4, 29.6}` are the min and max of a single `clamp()` on one element, not hierarchy steps. The real scale is 10px → 120px, a 12:1 ratio.

**Browser overlay.** Injection succeeded on all four pages (index 26 badges, gallery 42, contact 10, kimbia 30); mutation preflight confirmed by writing and reading back `document.title` and executing an appended script. Console reported 13/21/5/15 anti-patterns. The live server was stopped and the working tree verified clean.

Confirmed by the overlay beyond the contrast work: `.shot` is a `<button>` and the stylesheet never sets `font-family` on it, so gallery captions fall to the UA default and render **Arial 13.33px** — there is no `button { font: inherit }` anywhere. `.shot__tag` is 10px, below the 11px floor. Three footer strings are `text-transform: uppercase` runs of 37/49/31 characters. All hero-region contrast flags are false positives: the backdrop is a full-bleed `<img>` at `z-index: -2`, invisible to a `background-color` ancestor walk.

## Overall Impression

The top 900px of the homepage is genuinely good work — better than most athlete sites will ever be. Everything downstream is a well-built shell around content that isn't there yet. The single biggest problem is not visual: **every high-intent action on this site terminates in a placeholder, and does so confidently rather than visibly.** Donate copies six X's and says "Copied". Contact discards the message and says "received". The media kit button animates on hover and downloads nothing. A sponsor or journalist doesn't experience that as "unfinished" — they experience it as broken or dishonest.

## What's Working

1. **The hero typographic system.** The scale is large enough to become a poster rather than a headline; −0.02em tracking stops Fraunces' high-contrast serifs going airy at 108px; the two-tone span/em break puts gold on the payload word. It survives the drop to 390px intact.
2. **The `.stats` strip construction.** Hairlines from `gap: 1px` over a translucent background rather than borders — no double edges, no `border-collapse` workaround — with 52%-opacity mono meta text giving it the texture of a results board.
3. **Gold as information, never ornament.** Six roles only. Most athlete sites lose this in week one and end up gilded.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The donate path dead-ends in a placeholder with no alternative route.**
`.donate` on kimbia-na-simbu.html: the QR reads "M-PESA QR CODE (COMING SOON)", `.lipa__value` is `XXXXXX`, `[data-copy="XXXXXX"]` copies six X's and reports "Copied", and `.steps` instructs the user to scan or enter it. Both actions are impossible.
*Why it matters:* donors are a confirmed audience and this is the only money-attached conversion on the site. It fails silently — the confident "Copied" is what a real donor acts on.
*Fix:* replace the grid with one honest panel — "M-Pesa giving opens [month]. To give now, email info@alphoncesimbu.com" — with a live `mailto:` carrying a prefilled subject. Delete the Copy button and the five steps rather than documenting a route that doesn't exist. Keep the 40/30/20/10 allocation; that part persuades.
*Command:* `/impeccable clarify`

**[P0] No AI-generation disclosure on synthetic imagery of a real living person, and one image contradicts its own alt text.**
All nine images are AI depictions captioned with event-specific claims. The gallery medal is alt-texted "World Championship Gold Medal, Tokyo 2025" while **the rendered medal reads BUDAPEST 23**. There is no "AI-generated", "illustration", or "depiction" string anywhere in any file.
*Why it matters:* press is a confirmed audience whose stated need is verified facts and usable imagery. A journalist who runs one of these and later learns it is synthetic is a catastrophic outcome for Simbu personally. The Budapest/Tokyo mismatch is what a picture desk catches in ten seconds.
*Fix:* a persistent line in the gallery header and every figcaption — "Illustrative image, AI-generated. Not a photograph. Not for editorial use." Strip event-specific claims from alt text. Pull or re-caption the medal.
*Command:* `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Contact success is invisible, and the disclaimer under it is developer copy.**
`main.js` inserts `.form-status` at the top of the form and calls `form.reset()`; `role="status"` is set in the same tick the text is written, so assistive tech generally will not announce it. Directly beneath the submit button sits: *"This static build validates in the browser only — connect the form to an email service or API endpoint to deliver messages."*
*Why it matters:* this is the sponsor's and journalist's only route on the page, and it manages to look broken and admit it's fake, in that order.
*Fix:* move `.form-status` after the button, put `aria-live="polite"` in the markup rather than setting it at submit time, add `scrollIntoView` and programmatic focus. Replace the developer note with a real expectation.
*Command:* `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] The `.stat` strip misaligns and `.press` asserts coverage it cannot evidence.**
"2025 & 2026" wraps to two lines (82px vs 41px), dropping its label 41px below its three siblings' shared baseline — the first credibility artifact after the hero. Below it, eight news brands as unlinked list text with no logos, headlines, dates or links.
*Fix:* give `.stat` a flex column with `justify-content: space-between`, shorten to "2025 · 2026"; convert three or four mentions into linked, dated citations and delete the rest.
*Command:* `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Zero social share imagery on a site whose distribution is social shares.**
Verified zero occurrences of `og:image`, `twitter:card`, and `rel="canonical"` across all six files — while kimbia ships WhatsApp/X/Facebook share buttons. Every share renders as a naked grey text link, including the WhatsApp forward that is the actual distribution mechanism for a Tanzanian donor.
*Fix:* `og:image` (1200×630, the finish-line frame), `og:image:alt`, `og:url`, `twitter:card=summary_large_image`, canonical — six lines per file. Highest leverage-per-byte item on this list.
*Command:* `/impeccable harden`

## Minor Observations

- `.timeline` is 896px inside a 1240px shell on career.html — 344px of dead right column.
- The newsletter `.form-status` uses a gold-tinted box styled for the cream page; on `--ink-deep` it reads as a caution box, and it inserts *above* the input, shifting the field and button down on success.
- `.press__list li:hover { color: var(--ink) }` — a hover state on non-interactive text, which reads as a broken link.
- The footer duplicates the entire 6-item nav; on mobile that is 12 identical targets in one scroll.
- `© 2026` and "Boston 2026 / April 2026" sit alongside "2025 World Champion" — the site is dated a year past its own headline.
- The homepage portrait's alt is "Alphonce Simbu portrait"; the same file in the gallery is "Portrait — the focus of a World Champion". One asset, two voices.
- `.qr` correctly carries `role="img"` with a descriptive `aria-label` — a thoughtful touch on an element that did not need one, which makes the entirely unstyled `disabled` button more surprising.
- `.head p` (0,1,1) outranks `.eyebrow` (0,1,0), so any eyebrow inside a `.head` block silently loses its accent role and renders muted ink. Not a contrast failure; a system inconsistency.

## Questions to Consider

1. The metadata says "Official website"; the contact page says "This static build validates in the browser only". Which would you rather a sponsor's counsel read first — and if the answer is neither, why is the fix "edit the meta tag" rather than "say on the homepage that this is a proposal built for his team"?
2. Every high-intent action here ends in a placeholder or a discarded submission. What is the conversion event you actually want? If it is "they email info@", why is that address a 12px footer link instead of the hero's second button?
3. You have committed to imagery depicting a real man winning a real race, generated rather than photographed, captioned with date and venue. If Simbu's management sees this page before you do, is the AI imagery the thing that impresses them or the thing that ends the conversation?
4. The Kiswahili button translates 2 of ~40 text blocks. Is a toggle that does that more respectful of a Tanzanian donor than no toggle at all, or less?
5. Three PBs read TBC and the media kit does not exist. Is it better to ship the table half-empty, or to ship only the marathon PB so the absence reads as a choice rather than a gap?
6. The strongest 900px on this site is the homepage hero; career, gallery and contact all open with a flat colour block and never recover. Why did the hero get the entire design budget?
