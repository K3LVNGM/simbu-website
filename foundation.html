Our Mission

Every champion was once a child with a dream and someone who believed

The Alphonce Simbu Foundation was born from a simple truth: talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. Growing up in Arusha, Alphonce knows firsthand that the road from red-dirt training paths to the world stage requires more than just fast legs — it takes community, resources, and unwavering support.

The Foundation works to ensure that no young Tanzanian athlete is held back by circumstance. Through training programs, educational scholarships, and grassroots community development, we're building a future where the next World Champion might come from any village in Tanzania.

What We Do

Our Programs

Youth Athletics

Training programs for young runners across Arusha and northern Tanzania, providing coaching, equipment, and race entry support.

Education Scholarships

Scholarships for student-athletes to pursue secondary and higher education while developing their athletic potential.

Community Empowerment

Community sports facilities, health awareness programs, and grassroots athletics events that bring people together through running.

Equipment & Infrastructure

Providing running shoes, training gear, and improving local training facilities to give young athletes the tools they need.
